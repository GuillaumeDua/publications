# Code of Conduct

Interactions on this blog — including comments, reactions, and discussions
via [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/GuillaumeDua/publications/discussions) — are governed by the
[Berlin Code of Conduct](https://berlincodeofconduct.org/).

## Summary

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance,
body size, race, age, religion, or nationality.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in your language and actions
- Be constructive when giving feedback or criticism
- Be welcoming to newcomers and people with different backgrounds or experience levels
- Assume good faith in others' contributions

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Offensive comments related to personal characteristics
- Deliberate misgendering or use of rejected names
- Gratuitous or off-topic sexual content
- Threats or incitement of violence
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/GuillaumeDua/publications/issues) (make sure the issue name starts with `[CoC]`)
or contacting the author directly via the contact information on the [About](/publications/about/) page.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in comments or reactions being removed, and the offending
GitHub account being blocked from further interactions on this repository.

## Full Text

The full Berlin Code of Conduct is available at [berlincodeofconduct.org](https://berlincodeofconduct.org/).
