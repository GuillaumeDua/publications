## COMMENTS AND REACTIONS

This blog uses [Giscus](https://giscus.app) to power comments and reactions.
Giscus stores comments and reactions in this blog's [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/GuillaumeDua/publications/discussions).

If you post a comment or reaction, your **GitHub username, profile picture, and message (among other datas linked to your Github profile) are publicly visible** in the linked GitHub Discussions page, and are subject to
[GitHub's Privacy Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Anonymous reactions are not stored. No comment or reaction data is collected or processed
by this blog directly.
